# Transportation

In the Above, getting around can be tricky. Each Above island is separated by massive voids and your elytra wont work. Fortunately there are many ways of getting around.

## Footpaths

Throughout the Above islands are walking paths. While on these paths, you will get a slight movement speed buff. `Does this also apply to ridden horses?`

## Gateways

At the edges of islands are gateways that connect to a similar portal across the gap between islands. Each gateway has a material block above it which indicates the destination.

## Horses

At the High Horse™ stables in the Above, you can lease a horse to help you move around. You will need to give the [Stablemasters](/NPCs/Stablemasters) 1 [Titanshard Gem](/Loot#gems) as a deposit and a horse will spawn with random colored armor. Horses are bound to the player that got it. 

Once you are finished you can return the horse to any High Horse™ stable and get your deposit back.

Failure to return the horse will result in needing to ring the Bell of Shame before another horse can be leased.

Abandoned horses can be returned to a stablemaster by another player for a partial return of the original deposit.

## Ancient Mineshafts

Around the Above are a series of interconnected, non-euclidean mineshafts. Upon entering their mazelike halls, players may become momentarily disoriented and find themselves exiting the mines far from where they entered.

Below the village is a hub area with many passageways. Each bears indication of a material block corresponding to its ultimate destination. These passageways are unlocked per-player and allow fast travel between islands.

In the hub, unlocked pathways have a sparkle effect on the lantern. Unlocking all 16 will grant the Superior Spelunker advancement.

## Sunken Tunnels

Many islands also have a cave filled with water that leads to a dead end with a bubbling conduit. Interacting with the conduit will transport players nearby into an underwater hub area.

In the hub area there are many paths, each with a material block indicating their ultimate destination. Just like the Ancient Mineshaft shortcuts, the sunken tunnel shortcuts must be unlocked per-player.

Unlocking all 14 sunken tunnel shortcuts will grant the Dedicated Diver advancement.

## Mycelial Network

Active only at night, fairy rings will allow travel between four distinct locations that line up with the four cardinal compass directions. The spruce forest mountain in the north, the swamp in the east, the desert in the south, and the Dark Oak Forest in the west. The northern location acts as a hub for the Mycelial Network, providing two-way teleportation between the hub and the other three locations.

## Other ways

Chorus fruit and ender pearls will work in some, but not all, places of the Above. Because you are in adventure mode you may get stuck and need a Mod to get you unstuck so caution is recommended. Players may jump into the void to return to the overworld, then relaunch themselves into The Above as the fastest way to the Village from an island on the outskirts.
