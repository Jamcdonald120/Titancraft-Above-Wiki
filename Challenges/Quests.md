## Rescue the Archaeologist
### Description
An archeologist who's more of a bookworm than a field worker has managed to get themselves burried under a digsite and they need rescuing. You'll need to dig them up.
### How to complete
Head to the [desert island](/..Islands/Desert) and locate the digsite. `Need the rest of the quest line`
### What will cause failure
Leaving the area once you've heard their cries for help will be treated as abandoning them and will fail the quest
