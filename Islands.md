# Islands

The Above is made of many islands that float high above the clouds of the Titancraft overworld. Each island has a unique biome, challenges, and adventures.

As elytra are no longer effective in the Season 11 Above, to traverse the space between islands players must walk through a series of gateways at surface level that will instantaneously teleport them across great distance.

There are also shortcuts to discover, accessed through ancient mineshafts or underwater caves. See [transportation](../transportation.md) for more info

Each island has a material block associated with it.
