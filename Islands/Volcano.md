# Volcano
Associated Material Block :- Blackstone

## Description
A tall and active volcano occupies the northwest corner of The Above. There are multiple lava flows and pools making navigating around the volcano quite hazardous. Further from the volcano itself, the island has fragmented into many smaller islands that are only loosely connected by organic tendrils of red nether infection
