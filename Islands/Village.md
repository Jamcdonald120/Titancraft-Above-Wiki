# The Village
Associated Material Block :- Stripped Oak Wood

## Description
A medium sized [island](../Islands) that serves as the player's entry point into The Above. The island is a sparse forest with many flowers, farmland, two small lakes and at the center of the island is a walled village. The village features a number of houses and workplaces for its NPC inhabitants, most of whom will help the player with their adventures in some way or another.

Under the central tree is the Well, where players can throw in [Titanshard Gems](../Loot#gems) in the hopes for getting some exclusive Above [loot](../Loot).

The village walls have three taller watchtowers at the main gates, each of which has a guard at the top. The northeastmost tower has either [Victor or Victoria](../NPCs/VictorVictoria), who appear to have a slightly strained family dynamic.
