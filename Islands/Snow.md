# Snow Mountain Island
Asociated Material Block :- Ice

## Description
A medium sized island in the northeast corner of The Above. This island features three mountain peaks, each covered in deep snow. Frozen rivers and lakes dot the landscape. In a low lying central region there's an ice spikes sub-biome. Spruce forest borders the outskirts of the island, mostly under a layer of snow, but the westmost region is snow free.

Just to the south, connected by two rope bridges, is a smaller spruce forest island that similarly is half covered in snow
