# Loot

## Titanshards 
### Splinters

The most frequent loot in the Above is the Titanshard Splinter. These cannot be redeemed on their own and must be converted to Titanshard Gems first. [Morgana](npcs/Morgana), the cleric of the Shrine of the Shattered Shard, will convert 9 Splinters into 1 Gem for you. Wandering Traders may offer a better exchange rate.

### Gems

Titanshard Gems are the highly sought after currency on Titancraft. They can be exchanged for a random reward at the Well in the Village, or exchanged for specific items with various traders around the Above.


## The well

In the Village is the well. The well allows players to turn their Titanshard Gems into exclusive loot. To use the well, spin the arrow to point to the category of item you want, throw in the corresponding amount of gems, and pull the lever! Specific categories of items require 5 Gems be thrown into the well, while fully random requires only 3
