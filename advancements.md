# Advancements


Titancraft boasts a fun array of additional advancements for players to achieve, the Above is no different. This page lists the advancement name and flavor text, then a description of how to complete each one if you click to expand them.

<details><summary>The Above - Enter the Above</summary>You clicked the spoiler section for this? Really?</details>
<details><summary>While Supplies Last - Redeem a coupon</summary>Optain a coupon, as loot, as reward, or in exchange for Titanshard Gems and use it. Check the library for coupon specific instructions</details>
<details><summary>Where Credit's Due - Read all of the credits</summary>Find and read all of the ingame credits. These are located on the Village Island, underneath the chapel, look for an iron trapdoor outside at the base of the wall</details>
<details><summary>Run Away - Jump off the Above, back to the Overworld</summary>Run to the edge of any island and yeet yourself into the void</details>
<details><summary>Finders Keepers - Who doesn't like free stuff?</summary>Open a loot chest or barrel and claim what's contained within (only works with freshly reset loot)</details>
<details><summary>Serial Finder - Open 1000 loot chests and barrels</summary>Only counts freshly reset chest/barrel openings, if someone else got there first, they got the credit</details>
<details><summary>Fish your Wish - Fish at a lucky spot in the Above</summary>Use a fishing rod to successfully snag some loot from a fishing spot. These look like a ring of bubbles in the water</details>
<details><summary>Just Follow the Needle - Sherlock Holmes has got nothing on you!</summary>Follow a scavenger compass to its loot! Simply hold the compass in your main hand and stand on the block it points to.</details>
<details><summary>A Friendly Face - Not everyone here wants to kill you</summary> Talk to one of the NPCs<br></br>
- Does this have any limitations? villagers? traders? stablemasters?</details>
<details><summary>Go Quest, Young Man! - Complete any quest</summary>Quests are given by Victor and Victoria, go talk to them, follow their instruction and return to them aterwards</details>
<details><summary>No Further Quest-ions - Complete all quests</summary>There are 8 total</details>
<details><summary>Happens To The Best of Us - Sometimes you just can't help the RNG you know?</summary>Fail a quest<br></br>
- needs confirmation</details>
<details><summary>It's a Deal! - Trade with any of the Above's premier sales people</summary>Trade with a Wandering Trader, you'll need to bring some Titanshard Gems or Splinters with you</details>
<details><summary>Second Pickings - Purchase some unsold stock</summary>Be the second (or later) player to buy something from a Wandering Trader on any given day</details>
<details><summary>Magnificent Merchants - Trade with each type of the Above's traders</summary>There are 9 types, detailed on the Wandering Traders page</details>
<details><summary>Supporting the local Economy - Trade with the Above's traders 30 times</summary>I don't know to explain this any clearer</details>
<details><summary>An Apple a Day - Find and rescue Kovac</summary>Kovac is missing after falling to misadventure in the Snowy Mountains. Bring a golden apple and a splash potion of weakness to cure his zombie bite</details>
<details><summary>Meet & Greet - Meet everyone in the central village</summary>There are 16 villagers including 2 guards in towers, Victor and Victoria who swap places occasionally, and Kovac who returns when rescued</details>
<details><summary>Shard To Handle - Aquire a Titanshard Gem</summary>Exchange Titanshard Splinters with Morgana, with Bartholomew or Brooke. Or simply be given one rom another player, the only check is your inventory not your method of obtaining</details>
<details><summary>Spin the Wheel - Trade your Titanshard Gems or special loot</summary>Use the Well</details>
