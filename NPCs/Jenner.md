# Jenner

Jenner is a trader located in the Southeast of the spawn village that sells items lost at resetting Above encounters. Paying one Titanshard Gem to Jenner grants you a shulker box of random lost items, allowing players a chance to retrieve their items, but with no guarantee

On the inside of Jenner's house, there is a sign to the left of the exit that players can right click and "unpack" a shulker's contents into their inventory. This is not limited to shulkers acquired specifically from Jenner
