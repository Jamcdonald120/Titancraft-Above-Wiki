# Harriet

Harriet is a cartographer who lives in the south west of the Village in a wooden house. Harriet will sell the player a range of maps and compasses to help them navigate the Above.

## Wares

|Price|Item|Description|
|---|---|---|
|4 [Titanshard Gems](../loot.md#gems)|Above Map|A zoomed out overview of the entire Above. Does not show player location when held|
|1 [Titanshard Gem](../loot.md#gems)|Ancient Mineshaft Map|`Information Needed`|
|1 [Titanshard Gem](../loot.md#gems)|Flooded Cave Map|`Information Needed`|
|1 [Titanshard Gem](../loot.md#gems)|Trader Location Hint|These nine items appear as compasses but will be delivered as a text based hint to a specific [Wandering Trader](Traders.md#wandering-traders)'s current location. Traders appear in a randomised location at 6am each in-game day and disappear at 6pm. If a player tries to purchase a trader location hint before 6am, Harriet will refund their Titanshard Gem|
