# Kovac

Kovac is the blacksmith of the Village, their workshop is the ground floor of their house in the north east of the Village.

Kovac has got themself into trouble and is often not at their workshop in the Village. Players will have to find and rescue them

<details><summary>Quest spoilers</summary>Kovac is stuck in an igloo and has been bitten by a zombie. The igloo can be found on the Snowy Mountain island. Players will need to bring either a golden apple and a splash potion of weakness, or one gunpowder. There is a brewing stand inside Kovac's igloo contining a non-splash potion of weakness that the gunpowder can be added to, converting it to a splash potion. Brewing the potion reveals a ladder to the igloo basement, where a barrel contains the golden apple needed to finish the process. The method for healing Kovac is identical to standard zombie villagers</details>

Once Kovac is returned to the Village, he will offer to upgrade player's gear. Items that have Above exclusive enchantments can be converted to netherite through Kovac for a cost and items, from The Above or vanilla, can have their material altered by bringing Kovac a block of that material type. ie. A log to turn items wooden, a block of iron to turn an item iron, etc. Updating an Above-enchanted item to Diamond (if it's not already) will cost 3 Titanshard Gems. Updating an Above-enchanted item from Diamond to Netherite will cost 2 Titanshard Gems.
