# Victoria and Victor
These two senior [village](../Islands/Village) guards act as The Above's questgivers. Roughly once per real day, they will have a quest for players. There are currently 8 quests to complete which will take players all over The Above.

Neither will take a player who talks to them without a weapon in hand seriously, so take a sword when you go to get your quests, there's no place for sightseers in The Above!
