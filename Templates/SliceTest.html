Wither

The Wither is a player-constructed undead hostile boss mob that can hover in mid-air and fire explosive skulls at its targets.

It is one of the most powerful hostile mobs in Minecraft and is the only source of the Nether Star, which is used to craft a Beacon.

Behavior

The Wither is hostile toward most living mobs. It floats through the air and continuously seeks targets to attack.

When first summoned, the Wither charges for a short period before becoming active. During this time it is invulnerable.

Attacks

The Wither attacks by firing explosive Wither skulls. These projectiles can destroy blocks and inflict the Wither status effect.

Wither skulls

The Wither fires two different types of skulls. Normal skulls are dark, while rarer blue skulls have additional properties.

Attack Effect
Wither skull Explodes on impact and can inflict the Wither effect.
Blue wither skull A slower projectile capable of breaking certain blocks.
Dash The Wither can rapidly move toward targets.

Summoning

The Wither is summoned by arranging four blocks of Soul Sand or Soul Soil in a T shape and placing three Wither Skeleton Skulls on top.
