# Kovac

Kovac is the blacksmith of the Village, their workshop is the ground floor of their house in the north east of the Village.

Kovac has got themself into trouble and is often not at their workshop in the Village. Players will have to find and rescue them

<details><summary>Quest spoilers</summary>Kovac is stuck in an igloo and has been bitten by a zombie. The igloo can be found on the Snowy Mountain island. Players will need to bring a golden apple and a splash potion of weakness. The method for healing Kovac is identical to standard zombie villagers</details>
