# Morgana

Morgana is the cleric of the Shrine of the Shattered Shard. The Shrine can be found in the chapel in the east of the Village. If players drop [Titanshard Splinters](../loot.md#splinters) on the altar in front of Morgana, they will perform a ritual to convert the Splinters into [Titanshard Gems](../loot.md#gems). Morgana can convert multiple Gems worth of Splinters at once, as long as a multiple of nine Splinters are dropped on the altar.
