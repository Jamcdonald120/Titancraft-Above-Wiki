# Village Librarians

In the Village there are three librarians who work in the library. The library is central to the Village, built of stone and has stained glass windows.

The library also contains ender chests as well as lecturns with information about [Coupons](../loot.md#coupons)

## Giles

Giles is the main librarian who will share some basic information about the Above with the player

## Suggestion Sammy

Sammy stand on Giles' right and allows players to make [suggestions](https://forms.gle/h79WzutkCVK7df6J6) for future changes and additions to the Above.

## Bugreport Barry

Barry stands on Giles' left and allows players to [report bugs](https://github.com/Titancraft-Datapacking/Titancraft-BugTracker/issues) they've encountered
