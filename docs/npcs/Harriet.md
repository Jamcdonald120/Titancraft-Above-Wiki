# Harriet

Harriet is a cartographer who lives in the south west of the Village in a wooden house. Harriet will sell the player a range of maps and compasses to help them navigate the Above.

## Wares

|Price|Item|Description|
|---|---|---|
|4 [Titanshard Gems](../loot.md#titanshards)|Above Map|A zoomed out overview of the entire Above. Does not show player location when held|
|1 [Titanshard Gem](../loot.md#titanshards)|Ancient Mineshaft Map|`Information Needed`|
|1 [Titanshard Gem](../loot.md#titanshards)|Flooded Cave Map|`Information Needed`|
|1 [Titanshard Gem](../loot.md#titanshards)|Trader Compass|There are nine different compasses to purchase, each will take the player to a specific Wandering Trader's current location. Traders move at the start of each ingame day and disappear at night `Precise Wandering Trader spawning behaviour needs confirmation`|
