# Ice Cavern

![Ice Cavern](img/Kermode.png)

The snow filled tundra holds many secrets. Traversing this cold landscape can be harsh so finding shelter in a cave seems like a good idea. Not all caves are as save as they seem to be. If you stumble across this specific one you'll find [Kermode](bosses/kermode.md) has made her home here. Better keep your sword ready, this bear might be too much for just fists too handle... Or is she?
