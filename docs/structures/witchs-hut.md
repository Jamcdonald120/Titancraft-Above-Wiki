# Witch's Hut

![Witch's Hut](img/Witch_Hut.png)

Located in the Swamp you can find this crooked hut. Walking around there are lots of witches here, but they are just apprentices to the one hiding in the tower. [Gremora](bosses/gremora.md) is up to no good brewing all sorts of potions here. Will you fall to her magic or can you endure and come out victorious?
