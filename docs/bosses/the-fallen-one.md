# The Fallen One

The Fallen One has returned for his revenge.

## Spectre Souls

You will need to hunt for Spectres. Each Spectre will drop one soul that you will use to enter the arena. You will need three in total each one should be dropped on a pedestal (lodestone) to gain access to the next antechamber of The Fallen One's tower.

## Fight

After passing through the third gate you have time to prepare yourself for the fight ahead. The Fallen One fight is divided into three phases represented by the three boss bars.

While the boss bars are white they are invulnerable, you have to wait for the bars to turn red before attacking them. During this time Fallen Minions and phantoms will spawn.

Phases 1 and 3 are the same, kite and kill the mobs while waiting for The Fallen One to become vulnerable then use your sword to attack them.

For the second phase The Fallen One, when vulnerable, will only take damage from attacks from tridents dropped by the minions. The tridents can only be used once, if you pick up an already used trident it wont throw and you'll have to discard it.

If you die in The Fallen One fight will respawn in the antechamber of the arena.

## Advancements

Defeating the Fallen will earn you the [Break the Fallen](../advancements.md#break-the-fallen) achievement. If you managed to deal the final blow at every stage you will earn the [GloryHog](../advancements.md#glory-hog) achievement.
