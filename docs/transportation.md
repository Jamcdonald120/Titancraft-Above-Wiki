# Transportation

In the Above, getting around can be tricky. Each Above island is separated by massive voids and your elytra wont work. Fortunately there are many ways of getting around.

## Portals

At the edges of each island are portals that connect to a similar portal across the gap between islands.

## Paths

Throughout the Above islands are paths. On the paths, you will get a movement speed buff.

## Horses

At the stables on the spawn island, and elsewhere in the Above, you can get a horse to help you move around. You will need to give the merchant 1 Titanshard as a deposit and a horse will spawn with random colored armor. Horses are bound to the player that got it. 

Once you are finished you can return the horse to any stable and get your deposit back.

## Other ways

Chorus fruit and ender pearls will work in some, but not all, places of the Above. Because you are in adventure mode you may get stuck and need a Mod to get you unstuck so this isn't recommended.

There are still, as of yet, two other ways of getting around that haven't yet been discovered.
