## **Trivia**
- Ramses was originally created because Limbrogger felt it was strange to have such a prominent feature as the sphynx on the map with nothing inside it. Making the event span across several locations in the desert also provided several much needed points of interest on such a large and -at the time- empty island.
- Ramses is actually just a normal wither without any modified attributes. The arena is what makes the fight difficult.
- Ramses is named after [Ramsesses II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramesses_II), a celebrated Pharaoh of the New Kingdom period of ancient Egypt.
