## **Location**
Queen Bee  is a boss on the [Birch](../Islands/Birch) island. She is found under a larger birch tree in a honey cave.
You can tell if she will spawn if there are floating honey particles in the cave as you enter and you hear smaller bees popping out of their hives. She is notably larger than the other bees.

*Picture of Arena*

## **Fight**
After a short while in the honey cave/hive, the Queen Bee will spawn. During the fight with her, she will periodically spawn her own stronger larger bees as protection and also give herself some regen.

*Picture of individual Boss*

## **Strategy**
If you wish to fight in the nest, it's best to try and trap the Queen in a corner where you are still able to jump for critical attacks (don't stand on honey). The fight can lead to armor breaking very quickly due to the rapid attacks of the rest of the bees so it's best to occassionally sweeping edge the smaller bees for some mending value.

If you wish to, you can lead the bees including the Queen out of the nest as soon as they spawn. This can be easier to manage the fight in most cases as it gives you more space to run/kite and also alleviates getting stuck in the honey.

In both cases, it can be very beneficial to use Bane of Arthropods on a sword or axe to deal extra damage to the Queen and mitigate her regen. If using a sword, using Sweeping Edge is great for dealing with large amounts of the smaller weaker bees at once.
