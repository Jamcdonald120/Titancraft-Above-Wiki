## **Location**
Ragnok the Undying is a boss located on the [Spruce Island](../Islands/Spruce). They are found inside a cave on the south west of the island. The cave is hidden behind a tree, just past the river running down from the Midas cleansing pool. Once inside, head towards the glow berries on your left and you should see the location of the fight down a longer glow berry vine pictured below.

## **Fight**
Once you drop into the arena, there will be a glowing totem on a pedestal in the centre of the room. To start the fight, right click the totem and one of the four doors around the room will open and a timer will start.

![ragnok_totem.png](/ragnok_totem.png =960x)
Pictured: Totem on central pedestal

Each of the four rooms contains a different "puzzle" to obtain a block which you will need to bring back to the central room and place in it's respective pedestal in the corners. You can tell which pedestal relates to which block by the particles emitting from the pedestal.

![ragnok_corner_pedestal.png](/ragnok_corner_pedestal.png =960x)
Pictured: Diamond Block corner pedestal emitting blue particles

Diamond Room:
This room contains a small parkour course with a magma floor. There are also skeletons in the room making the parkour especially difficult without falling. This room is designed to waste time on the clock and make you take repeated tick damage from the magma. Retrieve the Diamond Block at the end of the room and bring it back to the central room.

![ragnok_diamond.png](/ragnok_diamond.png =960x)
Pictured: Diamond Block at end of parkour

Gold Room:
This room contains a handful of piglins protecting a Gold Block. The room is layed out so there is only one way to get access to the block with a couple dead-ends that become dangerous if you choose to run past the piglins. Retrieve the Gold Block at the end of the room and bring it back to the central room.

![ragnok_gold.png](/ragnok_gold.png =960x)
Pictured: Gold Block at end of room

Emerald Room:
This room contains an underwater section similar to the Gold Room. There will be 1 intended way to get access to the Emerald Block with dead-ends intended to waste time. Inside the water are pufferfish that give you a small amount of poison but more importantly knock you back quite heavily which also wastes time traversing the water. Retrieve the Emerald Block at the end of the room and bring it back to the central room.

![ragnok_emerald.png](/ragnok_emerald.png =960x)
Pictured: Emerald Block at end of room with pufferfish guarding

Copper Room:
This is the easiest of the rooms, layed out like a simple maze with silverfish inside. There are lots of winding paths that loop back on themselves intended to waste time. Retrieve the Copper Block at the end of the room and bring it back to the central room.

![ragnok_copper.png](/ragnok_copper.png =960x)
Pictured: Copper Block at end of room

Once you have obtained all 4 blocks and placed them in their respective pedestals, a short animation will play and Ragnok will spawn in the centre of the room.

Ragnok is a giant Stray wearing chainmail armor wielding a bow capable of shooting wind bursts. They are also holding a totem, and have 4 backup totems floating above their head giving a total of 6 health bars. Each health bar contains less total health than the last making the fight easier as time goes on.

![ragnok_fight.png](/ragnok_fight.png =960x)
Pictured: Ragnok mid-fight

## **Strategy**
Ragnok can be a tricky fight, especially if battling alone. Their wind burst bow makes it extremely difficult to close the gap and deal damage. Ragnok is also immune to projectiles so there's no bow combat or crossbow rocket potential.

It is recommended to bring a Smite weapon to deal extra damage when you get the chance to attack. Ragnok also has a small amount of natural regen that means you need to keep up attack pressure to stop healing.

For fighting Ragnok solo, it is recommended to do circles around them in the centre of the room. This allows you to consistently do damage, also critical hits if jumping at the right time, while also forcing Ragnok to miss their bow shots.

If you have armor enchanted with [Steady](../Enchantments/Steady) you can negate most, if not all, of the knockback from the wind bursts in the fight making it much easier to deal consistent damage and negate Ragnok's natural regen.
