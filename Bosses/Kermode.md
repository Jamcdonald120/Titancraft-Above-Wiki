## **Location**
Kermode is a boss on the [Snow Mountain island](../Islands/Snow). They live in an icy cave at the base of the mountain in the northwest corner of the island.

*Picture of Arena*

## **Fight**
When Kermode is alive, their cave shimmers with icy particles. The player's body temperature will begin to lower as they venture through it, as indicated by a blue bar atop their screen. Standing still will slowly lower the temperature, and being in the water or powder snow will quickly lower it. Running or jumping will slowly raise it. When their temperature gets too low, players will begin taking constant freezing damage until it's raised.

Small Breezes will also spawn throughout the cave to assist Kermode. They only have one health.

Kermode, who tends to hide at the end of the cave, will instantly attack any players they see. They otherwise behive identically to regular Polar Bears.

When Kermode is defeated, all Breezes will instantly die and the cave will warm up, letting players explore the cave without needing to watch their temperature.

*Picture of individual Boss*

## **Strategy**
Kermode doesn't hit very hard or have a lot of health compared to other bosses in The Above, which makes fighting them relatively easy even without strong armor. Repeatedly damaging them with critical sword swings will both keep Kermode from attacking and keep the player's body temperature up.

The spawning Breezes can serve to disrupt this strategy, but otherwise aren't very threatening. Kermode's low health means it's likely they won't even have time to spawn before Kermode is defeated, assuming the player is fighting with a weapon.

There is Powder Snow dotted around the arena. Player's may opt to bring leather boots to prevent getting stuck in it.

## **Trivia**
- Defeating Kermode without a weapon (only using fist punches) will reward players the ***With my Bear Hands*** advancement.
