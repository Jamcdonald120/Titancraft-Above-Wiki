## **Location**
The Broodmother is a boss on the [Dark Oak island](../Islands/DarkOak). She is a giant spider who lives in a cobweb filled cave connected to a cave system around the center of the island.

*Picture of Arena*

## **Fight**
Broodmother's arena exists separately to the cave system it's connected to, and players are simply teleported in and out of the arena when they reach certain positions. Whether a player is explicitly in or out of the arena determines whether or not Broodmother performs her attacks.

Broodmother behaves like a normal spider would, but also has 2 special attacks she alternates between. The first attack, telegraphed via 3 consecutive charging sounds, traps the player in a cage of cobwebs that disappears after a few seconds. The second attack, untelegraphed, has her teleport to a random position in the arena. When she does this, she is briefly given the status effects Glowing and Regeneration. Any "Broodspawn" in the arena at this time are also teleported, either to her current position or to a player's position.

Broodspawn are cave spiders that endlessly spawn throughout Broodmother's lair as long as she is alive. They have a chance of dropping a cobweb when defeated.

Once Broodmother is defeated, the Broodspawn instantly stop spawning. Any spawn that are still in the lair do not die with her, and will need to be defeated separately.

*Picture of individual Boss*

## **Strategy**
Broodmother has a lot of health, so player's wishing to fight her may want to bring a high level **Bane of Arthropods** sword. **Sweeping Edge** will also help deal with large swarms of Broodspawn. The longer the fight takes, the more Broodspawn will fill the lair, so dealing with Broodmother quickly is a top priority.

Her lair is very jagged and players will need to chase her down when she teleports. **Wind Charges** or **Ender Pearls** can greatly help navigate the arena quickly. Pearls have the added benefit of helping players escape cobwebs, an essential skill to have given her cobweb cage attack is otherwise completely unavoidable.

When Broodmother teleports, players should observe the arena to figure out where she teleported, and act fast to catch up to her. It's important to minimize the amount of time Broodmother spends isolated and regenerating.
