## **Location**
Volt is a boss on a [lone island northwest of the starting village](../Islands/LightningRod). They rest idle atop a hill, shattered and unmoving.

## **Fight**
Volt needs to be repaired and awakened before they are fought. To repair them, players must harvest 3 Electrum Ingots from the 3 small golems Ohm, Amp, and Watt. These golems randomly wander the island, and only fight back when struck.

Once repaired, Volt will briefly charge up and fully awaken with a strike of lightning. Afterward, they will quickly and aggressively chase nearby players to hit them with their powerful melee attacks. These attacks have a chance to fling players very high in the air, which can be very deadly to players unable to soften the landing.

Occasionally, Volt will jump high into the air, either as means of surprising the player upon landing or to move back to their original position when lured too far away.

While Volt is active, they will send electrical charges to the various lightning rods scattered around the arena. Charged lightning rods flicker with electricity and will instantly strike lightning when a player approaches them.

Like regular Iron Golems, Volt will visually crack as they take damage, which players can use to gauge how healthy Volt is.

## **Rewards**
On defeat, Volt drops 24 [Titanshard Splinters](../Loot#splinters) and a single Poppy that holds a message in it's lore.

## **Strategy**
Many of Volt's attacks deal very high damage, so players wishing to fight them will want to bring good armor. A way to get away from Volt for healing purposes is also recommended, as Volt is very fast and can easily keep up with players sprinting away from them.

Players should assume any lightning rods in the arena will be charged once Volt is awake, and should make an effort to stay away from them, as the lightning strikes they call deal very high damage.

When flung high in the air by Volt's melee attack, players will need to plan a safe landing, as Feather Falling boots do not trivialize the damage and there are no natural safe landing points in Volt's arena. **Slow Falling potions** and **Ender Pearls** are the easiest ways to mitigate the damaged caused by this. Keeping Ender Pearls ready can also help players create distance between them and Volt if needed.

Brave players can alternatively take advantage of the height gained from being flung with a **Mace**. Landing a hit on Volt with a mace after being flung both deals very high damage to Volt, and fully resets the players accumulated fall damage. If enchanted with Wind Burst, players can chain high damage smash attacks repeatedly against Volt, a strategy which Volt doesn't have a direct way to counter.
